# Lab 02 - LLM in Everyday Life

## Objective
The objective of this laboratory activity was to apply Large Language Models (LLMs) to solve a real-world challenge faced by university students. The lab focused on using AI as a product development partner to generate ideas, analyze problems, and design innovative solutions.

## Overview
In this activity, students worked in groups to identify a common challenge experienced by university students and develop an AI-assisted product concept to address the issue. Large Language Models were used throughout the ideation and development process to support brainstorming, feature generation, and solution design.

## Tasks Completed
- Analyzed the assigned student challenge scenario.
- Identified the core problem and user requirements.
- Used an LLM to generate innovative solution ideas.
- Developed a product concept and product name.
- Defined key features and functionalities.
- Identified the target user group.
- Designed a visual prototype/mock-up of the proposed solution.
- Evaluated how AI can support real-world product development.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Copilot / ChatGPT
- Large Language Models (LLMs)
- GitHub
- Documentation and Design Tools

## Results and Evidence
The activity successfully demonstrated how AI can be used as a creative and problem-solving assistant. The developed product concept addressed a practical student challenge and incorporated features generated through AI-assisted brainstorming and analysis.

### Evidence
- Product concept documentation
- Screenshots of AI interactions
- Prototype or mock-up designs
- Group discussion outcomes

## Observations
- LLMs can generate creative and practical ideas within a short period.
- AI-assisted brainstorming helps explore multiple solutions quickly.
- Human judgment is still required to evaluate and refine AI-generated suggestions.
- Effective prompts produce more relevant and useful outputs.

## Challenges Faced
- Selecting the most suitable idea from multiple AI-generated suggestions.
- Refining product features to make them realistic and user-friendly.
- Ensuring that the final solution addressed the actual student problem.

## Learning Outcomes
Through this laboratory activity, I learned how Large Language Models can be applied beyond text generation to support innovation and product development. I gained practical experience in problem analysis, prompt engineering, AI-assisted ideation, solution design, and critical evaluation of AI-generated outputs. The lab also enhanced my creativity, critical thinking, and understanding of real-world applications of generative AI.

## Conclusion
This lab demonstrated the practical value of Large Language Models in addressing everyday challenges. By using AI as a development partner, it was possible to generate innovative ideas, design effective solutions, and understand the strengths and limitations of AI-assisted product development.
